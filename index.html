Why Terra & Co.

Every piece has a maker's name attached to it.

We work directly with a small roster of studios and workshops. No mass production, no filler collections — just objects built to be used daily and to age well.

  • Small batches — most pieces are made in runs of fewer than 200.
  • Honest materials — stoneware, linen, soy wax, recycled glass.
  • Fair pricing — makers set their own rates; we don't mark up beyond what keeps the lights on.
